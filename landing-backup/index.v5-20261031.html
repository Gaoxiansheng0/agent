Privacy and limits

Three questions to ask first.

What happens to my photo?

It travels over an encrypted connection to our server and to our AI provider, DeepSeek, for analysis.

  • We don't save the photo. Your account keeps the scores and notes.
  • We don't use photos to train AI.
  • The provider's own handling falls under its terms.
Read the privacy policy

Where does the advice come from?

Two parts, kept apart on purpose.

  • An AI reads the photo, writes the report and arranges your routine.
  • Product verdicts come from a written set of ingredient rules, each warning with a cited source.
  • The AI can't change a verdict.

What can't it tell me?

It's a cosmetic estimate from one photo, not a diagnosis.

  • It can't identify skin conditions or allergies.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding questions get "ask your doctor", not a yes.
  • For anything that worries you, see a doctor.

Questions

Before you start.

Something else? Write to hello@pearlwren.com.

What do I get?

A skin report from one selfie: your skin type, 9 measures from oil to fine lines, and ingredients worth looking for. Then a verdict on each product you add, for each person you add, and a morning and night routine built from what you own.

How should I read my results?

As an estimate from one photo, not a diagnosis. Each measure has a level from none to marked and a score where higher means fewer visible signs. Light, makeup and angle change the result, so compare tests taken the same way.

What happens to my photo?

It goes over an encrypted connection to our server and to our AI provider, DeepSeek, for analysis. We don't save it and don't use photos to train AI. Your account keeps the scores and notes. The details are in our privacy policy.

Which products can I add?

Scan the barcode, take a photo of the ingredient list, or paste it in. PearlKin looks barcodes up in its own product list and in Open Beauty Facts. When it reads a label photo, you check the ingredients before anything is judged.

How does it work for my family?

One account holds up to 6 people. Each has an age, a life stage such as pregnancy or breastfeeding if it applies, and any skin concerns, and every product shows a verdict for each of them. Anyone 13 or older can take a skin test; for younger children PearlKin checks products only.

How do the trial and billing work?

Subscriptions run through the iPhone app and Apple: $29.99 a year or $9.99 a month, each with 3 days free. Starting the trial needs a payment method on your Apple ID. When it ends, the plan renews automatically until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends or the next renewal and you won't be charged again. You can also delete your account and all its data from the app's settings.
