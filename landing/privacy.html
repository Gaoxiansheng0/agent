Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

PearlKin gives you an AI skin report from a selfie and checks which skincare products suit you and the people in your family. It is run by PearlWren ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. The short version: we don't keep your face photo on our servers. Our AI provider, DeepSeek, also receives it to analyze it, and processes data in mainland China. What we know about DeepSeek's handling is set out below.

Face photos

  • When you take a skin test, your photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to our AI provider, DeepSeek, which analyzes visible skin features (such as pores, oil, spots and fine lines) and returns scores and notes.
  • We do not store the photo. It exists in memory on our server only while it is being analyzed. We do not create face templates, faceprints or any data used to identify a person, and we do not use photos to train AI.
  • We do not analyze the faces of children under 13.

What DeepSeek receives, and what we know about its handling

  • For a skin test: the photo, the person's approximate age, pregnancy or breastfeeding status if you entered it, the photo conditions you chose and your questionnaire answers. Not your name, email address or account identifier.
  • For a routine suggestion: the person's age group, pregnancy or breastfeeding status, skin concerns, a summary of their latest skin report, and the names and ingredient lists of their products. Not your name, email address or account identifier.
  • For an ingredient label photo: the photo of the label.
  • Where: DeepSeek's privacy policy says it collects, processes and stores data in the People's Republic of China.
  • How long: DeepSeek's terms for developers don't state how long it keeps what apps like ours send it, or whether it may use that data to improve its models. Its privacy policy says it doesn't cover data that apps built on its platform send. So we can't promise that a photo is deleted on DeepSeek's side.
  • We send DeepSeek only what each analysis needs, and we do not authorize it to use your data for any other purpose. These descriptions reflect DeepSeek's published documents as of October 31, 2026.

What we keep in your account

  • Account: your email address, or an Apple ID identifier if you sign in with Apple (Apple may give us a private relay address).
  • Skin reports: the scores, skin type, estimated skin age and notes from each test, the photo conditions you selected and your questionnaire answers.
  • Family profiles you add: name or nickname, birth year, pregnancy or breastfeeding status and skin concerns, if you enter them.
  • Your shelf: the products you add, their ingredient lists and who uses them.

We keep these so your history follows you across devices and so we can show progress over time. Pregnancy status and skin concerns are sensitive; we use them only to tailor your results, send them to DeepSeek only as described above for that purpose, and never sell them.

Other information

  • Product lookups: barcodes are sent to our server and to Open Beauty Facts, an open product database. Photos of ingredient labels are read by our AI provider and not stored.
  • Product contributions: when a barcode isn't in any database, the name, barcode and ingredient list you confirm may be added to our product library for everyone. Unrecognized ingredient names are sent so we can improve our rules. These contain no personal information.
  • Reports to us: if you report a problem with a product, we receive the product details, your note and, only if you type it, your email.
  • Usage data: the app sends events such as "skin test completed" or "subscription screen viewed" with a random device identifier, your platform and app version. We use them to count usage and fix problems.
  • Technical data: our server sees your IP address with each request and uses it to prevent abuse; logs keep it only as a one-way hash.
  • Website: our website counts visits anonymously with a random identifier in your browser. It sets no advertising cookies. If you ask to be notified about the iPhone app, we keep your email for that one purpose.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; it receives your account identifier and Apple's purchase records, never your payment details.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • We don't take money from brands, and brands can't change results.
  • We don't use your photos or data to train AI models. (For what DeepSeek's terms do and don't say, see above.)

Children

PearlKin is for adults 18 and over. Children can be added as profiles by a parent; we do not analyze photos of children under 13 and do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Service providers

We share data only as described above with: our hosting provider (servers in Hong Kong), DeepSeek (AI analysis of skin photos and label photos, and routine suggestions; processing in mainland China), Resend (sign-in code emails), Open Beauty Facts (barcode lookups), and Apple and RevenueCat (subscriptions). Your information may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, Hong Kong and mainland China.

How long we keep data

Account data, reports, family profiles and shelf: until you delete them or your account. Usage events and request logs: up to 24 months. Problem reports: until resolved, then up to 24 months. Face photos and label photos: never stored on our servers. DeepSeek doesn't publish how long it keeps them; see above.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a family member, a product or your whole account at any time in the app (Me → Delete account). Account deletion removes your profiles, reports and shelf from our servers.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, at hello@pearlwren.com.
  • Depending on where you live (including California, Washington, Illinois and Texas), you may have additional rights over personal, health or biometric-related data. We honor these requests for all users and will not treat you differently for using them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
