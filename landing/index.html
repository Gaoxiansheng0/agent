Questions

How accurate is the AI skin analysis?

It's a cosmetic estimate from one photo, like a trained eye looking closely. Bare skin, even light and the same spot each time give the most consistent results. It's not a medical exam.

Is this medical advice?

No. PearlKin gives cosmetic and ingredient information. For skin conditions, pregnancy or medications, talk to your doctor.

What happens to my photo?

It's sent securely to our AI for analysis and then discarded. We never store your face. Your scores, family profiles and shelf are saved to your account.

Can I use it for my kids?

Yes, as profiles under your account. Teens 13 and up can take skin tests. For younger children we check the products they use but don't analyze their faces.

How is this different from Yuka or other scanner apps?

Those apps give a product one score for everyone. PearlKin reads your skin first, then tells you whether each product suits you, your teen, or you during pregnancy.
