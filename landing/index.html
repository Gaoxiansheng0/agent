Built to be trusted

Rules, not guesses

Verdicts come from fixed rules based on published guidance. AI only reads the label. Before launch, a board-certified US dermatologist will review the rules.

No scare words

We say "good fit" or "use with care", never "toxic". Every flag shows its source, so you can check it yourself.

Profiles stay on your phone

Ages, pregnancy and skin concerns never leave your device. We don't sell data or take money from brands.

Questions

Is this medical advice?

No. Family Shelf gives ingredient information to help you choose products. For skin conditions, pregnancy or medications, talk to your doctor.

Can my teen use it on their own?

Not yet. Accounts are for parents 18+. Your kids are profiles under your account, not users.

What ages do you support?

At launch: ages 8–12, 13–17 and adults, including pregnancy. Baby and toddler rules are next, and founding members get them first.

When do I get the app?

We plan to launch on iPhone in spring 2027. Founding members get early TestFlight access. If we don't launch, you get a full refund.
