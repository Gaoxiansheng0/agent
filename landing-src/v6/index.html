Privacy and limits

Who handles your photo.

Three parties touch a skin check. Here's what each one does with it.

PearlKin's server

Receives the photo over an encrypted connection, passes it on for analysis and doesn't save it. We don't use photos to train AI.

DeepSeek, our AI provider

Reads the photo and returns scores and notes. Its privacy policy says it processes data in China. Its published terms don't say how long it keeps what apps send it, so we can't promise it's deleted on their side.

Your account

Keeps the scores and notes, family profiles and your shelf. Delete it any time from the app's settings.

Where the notes come from

An AI reads the photo, writes the report and suggests a routine. Product notes come from a written list of ingredient notes by age, life stage and skin, and the AI can't change them. In the app, each note names the organization behind it.

What it can't tell you

A report is a cosmetic estimate from one photo, not a diagnosis. It can't identify skin conditions or allergies. Pregnancy questions get “ask your doctor”, not a yes. For anything that worries you, see a doctor.

Questions

Before you start.

Something else? Write to hello@pearlwren.com.

What do I get?

A skin report from one selfie: your skin type, 9 measures from oil to fine lines, and ingredients worth looking for. Then a note on each product you add, for each person you add, and a morning and night routine built from what you own.

How should I read my results?

As an estimate from one photo, not a diagnosis. Scores run from 0 to 100, higher meaning fewer visible signs. Confidence describes the photo, not medical accuracy: light, makeup and angle change the result, so compare tests taken the same way.

Who handles my photo?

Our server receives it and passes it to DeepSeek, our AI provider, for analysis; we don't save it or use it to train AI. DeepSeek says it processes data in China and doesn't publish how long it keeps what apps send. Your account keeps the scores and notes.

Which products can I add?

Scan the barcode, take a photo of the ingredient list, or paste it in. PearlKin looks barcodes up in its own product list and in Open Beauty Facts. When it reads a label photo, you check the ingredients before anything is noted.

Is the web app free?

Right now, yes: every feature, with no card, while we test it. The prices on this page are for the iPhone app, which isn't out yet.

How do I cancel, or delete my data?

Cancel an iPhone subscription in Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the next renewal. Deleting your account in the app's settings removes your data; it doesn't cancel an Apple subscription, so do both if you want both.
